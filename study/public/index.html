Step 1 of 6

How people read loan calculators

A short study, about seven minutes, on how people read the results of online loan eligibility calculators.

What you will do

Type in some monthly figures, your own or made-up ones. You will see what nine loan calculators would show for them, and answer a few questions.

It is anonymous

The study never asks for your name, email, phone number or gender, and keeps your age only as an age group. Your exact money figures are used only to work out the nine results, and are never stored.

What is kept: your answers, your money figures as ranges (for example ₹30,000–₹49,999), and how the nine results compared with each other. The anonymous answers will be published with the research. No one can be identified from them.

It is not a loan application

Nothing you type is sent to any lender. The nine results are worked out from the public eligibility calculators of nine Indian lenders, as they were tested in November 2026. They are not loan offers, and the lenders are not named.

Taking part is voluntary

You can stop at any time by closing this page. Nothing is saved until you press “Submit answers” on the last screen.

Like almost all websites, the website host keeps short-lived visit logs, which include IP addresses. The study never reads or stores them, and uses no cookies or tracking.
