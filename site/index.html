This site compares nine public personal-loan eligibility calculators from Indian banks, lenders and a loan marketplace. Please read this first.

  • Recreated, not live. The results here are worked out from rules we reconstructed by testing the public calculator pages between 14 and 19 November 2026. The sites may have changed since.
  • Not connected to any of these companies. This site is not made by, or connected to, any of them.
