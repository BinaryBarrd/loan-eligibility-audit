# Live re-check of both calculators: 14 November 2026

Done in a real browser (the Claude desktop app's built-in browser) on the public calculator pages.
Values were typed into the calculator fields, mostly by script in the page (the same method as
`reference/checker_audit.py`), and two by real keyboard typing. Nothing was submitted; no phone
number, PAN or OTP was entered. Every observation is in `live_check_2026-11-14.csv`, next to what
`site/rules.js` predicts for the values the site actually used.

**Result: 53 observations. 44 matched the rules as they were; 3 are Bajaj range errors (no amount to
compare); 6 differed, all for one reason (Paisabazaar's minimum age, below). After review, the
minimum age of 21 was added to `reference/rules.py` and `site/rules.js`; all 53 now agree.**
(Three of the 53, tenure 6, 7 and 10 years, were added later the same day to back the "tenure
silently capped" finding on the page.)

## New things the original audit did not record

1. **Paisabazaar's minimum age is 21, not 18.** Ages 18, 19 and 20 show "Unfortunately, you may not be
   eligible for most loan offers , as your age is lower than the minimum age required." Age 21 passes.
   Confirmed by keyboard typing as well. The age check comes before the debt check. (None of the 76
   original probes used an age below 26.) **Added to both rule files after review.**
2. **Paisabazaar snaps typed values to the slider ends** and calculates with the snapped value:
   age 17 → 18, 71 → 70; desired ₹40,000 → ₹50,000, ₹60 lakh → ₹50 lakh; rate 7% → 8%, 31% → 30%;
   tenure 0 → 1, 11 → 10; income ₹15,000 → ₹20,000, ₹15 lakh → ₹10 lakh.
3. **Paisabazaar existing EMI is not capped at the slider's ₹40,000.** ₹45,000 and ₹90,000 were kept and
   used (results match the rule). But an EMI above the monthly income was changed to equal the income
   (income ₹40,000: ₹45,000 and ₹50,000 both became ₹40,000). That case is "not eligible" either way.
4. **Paisabazaar's tenure has a "Mo / Yr" switch** (months or years). We only tested years.
5. **Bajaj's page now names inputs the calculator does not ask for:** "estimate your eligible loan amount
   based on your income, expenses, age and repayment capacity", and "Enter your city, date of birth,
   monthly income and monthly expenses". There is no age or date-of-birth field.
